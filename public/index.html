YOUR GUIDE TO VICTORY!

Objective

Here is Tornad.IO! The objective of the game is to gain your tornado strength from destroying houses and trees. You then have an incoming of storm chasers and civilians. Dodge the civilians to avoid taking costly coin and strength damage! Storm chasers give you coins and remove tornado strength. Use those coins to buy OP upgrades and survive 3 minutes to achieve victory in tornad.io!

Perks

Once the upcoming upgrades have been purchased: Press "Q" for speed boost. Press "E" for size surge.
